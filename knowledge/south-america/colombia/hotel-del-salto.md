# Hotel del Salto

## Overview

The Hotel del Salto stands on the edge of a cliff facing the Tequendama Falls, where the Bogotá River drops about 150 metres into a deep gorge, some 30 km south-west of Bogotá. The French-style mansion was built in the 1920s as a grand country house for the capital's wealthy, who came to admire the falls, and it later became a hotel with a restaurant. As Bogotá grew, the river became badly polluted by the city's sewage and industry, the smell drove the guests away, and the hotel closed in the early 1990s. Standing empty in the mist above the falls, it gained a reputation as one of Colombia's most haunted buildings, and stories about it are still told today. In 2013 the building was restored and reopened as the Casa Museo del Salto del Tequendama, a museum about the biodiversity and culture of the region and the fight to clean up the river.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from El Dorado International Airport (BOG), about 35 km, allow 1–1½ hours through Soacha, depending on Bogotá's traffic.
- **Taxi:** taxis and private drivers can be hired at the airport or in Bogotá for a half-day trip.

## Visiting

The museum is open most days with an entry fee; check opening times before you go. The viewpoint over the falls is next to the building. Mist often covers the gorge, so visit early in the day for the best chance of a clear view.
