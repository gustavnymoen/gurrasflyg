# Kayaköy

## Overview

Kayaköy, once known as Levissi, is a village of several hundred stone houses climbing a hillside in a valley near Fethiye on the south-west coast of Türkiye. For centuries it was home to a Greek Orthodox community of several thousand people, who lived alongside Turkish neighbours in the surrounding area. After the Greco-Turkish War, the 1923 population exchange between Greece and Turkey forced its Greek-speaking inhabitants to leave for Greece. Muslims sent from Greece were meant to replace them, but few settled, and an earthquake in 1957 damaged many of the houses. The village has been empty ever since. Today the roofless houses, churches and chapels stand in rows on the slopes, with views over the pine-covered valley, and the site is protected as an open-air museum. It inspired the setting of Louis de Bernières' novel Birds Without Wings.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from Dalaman Airport (DLM), about 60 km, allow an hour via Fethiye.
- **Bus:** minibuses (dolmuş) run from Fethiye to Kayaköy in about 20 minutes.

## Visiting

The site is open daily with an entry fee. The paths between the houses are steep and stony, and there is little shade, so mornings and late afternoons are best in summer. Please respect the churches and chapels.
