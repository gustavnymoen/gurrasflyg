# Bhangarh Fort

## Overview

Bhangarh is a ruined fortified town in the Aravalli hills of Rajasthan, in the Alwar district between Jaipur and Delhi. It was founded in the late sixteenth century by a Rajput ruler for his son. Inside its walls lie the remains of a bazaar lined with shops, temples, a palace on the hillside, gardens and grand houses, with the hills rising behind. The town declined in the eighteenth century, probably because of famine and the loss of its strategic importance, and was eventually abandoned. Local legends say it was cursed by a sorcerer or a holy man, and Bhangarh is often called the most haunted place in India. The Archaeological Survey of India, which protects the site, forbids entry between sunset and sunrise, a rule that has only added to its reputation. Today it is a popular day trip, with well-preserved temples and atmospheric ruins.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from Jaipur International Airport (JAI), about 90 km, allow about two hours.

## Visiting

The site is open from sunrise to sunset with an entry fee; entry at night is forbidden. Monkeys live among the ruins, so keep food out of sight. Bring water, especially in the hot season.
