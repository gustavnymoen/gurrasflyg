# Kuldhara

## Overview

Kuldhara is an abandoned village in the Thar Desert, about 18 km west of Jaisalmer in Rajasthan, north-west India. It was founded centuries ago by Paliwal Brahmins, a community known for its skill in farming the dry land, and was one of a group of more than eighty Paliwal villages in the area. In the nineteenth century its inhabitants left, together with the people of the neighbouring villages, and never returned. Why they left is uncertain. Historians point to drought, heavy taxes and conflict with the rulers of Jaisalmer, while a popular legend says the villagers left in a single night to escape a cruel minister and cursed the village so that nobody could ever live there again. Today the roofless sandstone houses, laid out along straight streets, are protected as a heritage site, and a few houses and the temple have been restored.

## Getting there

- **Plane:** Jaisalmer Airport (JSA), about 20 km away, has limited flights. Jodhpur (JDH), about 300 km away, has more.
- **Car or taxi:** about 30 minutes from Jaisalmer.

## Visiting

The village is open during the day with a small entry fee and closes at sunset. Summer is extremely hot, so the best time to visit is from October to March. A visit often combines with a night in a desert camp.
