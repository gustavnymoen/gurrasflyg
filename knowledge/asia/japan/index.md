# Japan

Destinations:

- [Ashio Copper Mine](ashio-copper-mine.md) — A historic copper mine in the mountains of Nikkō, the site of Japan's first major pollution disaster. *Access: Open.*
- [Hashima](hashima.md) — An abandoned coal-mining island off Nagasaki, nicknamed 'Battleship Island' for its silhouette. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Kitazawa Flotation Plant](kitazawa-flotation-plant.md) — Overgrown concrete ruins of a gold-ore processing plant on Sado Island, part of a UNESCO World Heritage Site. *Access: Open.*
- [Okunoshima](okunoshima.md) — A small Japanese island with ruins of a secret wartime poison gas factory, and hundreds of wild rabbits. *Access: Open.*
