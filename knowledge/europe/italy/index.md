# Italy

Destinations:

- [Bussana Vecchia](bussana-vecchia.md) — A Ligurian hill village ruined by an earthquake in 1887 and resettled by artists. *Access: Open.*
- [Consonno](consonno.md) — A Lombardy hamlet rebuilt in the 1960s as a fantasy resort, the 'Las Vegas of Brianza', then abandoned. *Access: Open.*
- [Craco](craco.md) — A medieval hilltop town in southern Italy abandoned after landslides, visited on guided tours. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Pentedattilo](pentedattilo.md) — A ghost village in Calabria built beneath a rock shaped like a giant hand. *Access: Open.*
