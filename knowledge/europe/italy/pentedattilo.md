# Pentedattilo

## Overview

Pentedattilo is an abandoned village in the far south of Calabria, a few kilometres inland from the Ionian coast near Reggio Calabria. Its houses cling to the foot of a huge sandstone rock whose five pinnacles look like the fingers of a giant hand, which gave the village its Greek name, meaning "five fingers". The village is remembered for a bloody feud in 1686, when members of the ruling Alberti family were murdered by a rival baron. A major earthquake in 1783 damaged many buildings, and over the following centuries the inhabitants gradually moved to a new village closer to the sea, until the old one was completely empty by the 1960s. Since the 1980s volunteers and local associations have restored some of the houses, which now hold craft workshops and small exhibitions, and a short-film festival brings the village to life each summer.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from Reggio Calabria Airport (REG), about 30 km, allow 35 minutes. The last part is a narrow mountain road. There is no public transport to the village.

## Visiting

The village is freely accessible. Some restored houses with craft shops are open, mainly in summer. The paths are steep and uneven, so wear good shoes. Please respect the restoration work and do not climb on the rock.
