# Consonno

## Overview

Consonno was once a small farming hamlet on a hillside in the Brianza area near Lecco, north-east of Milan. In 1962 a businessman, Count Mario Bagno, bought the whole village and demolished most of it to build a fantasy resort he called the "city of toys", promising a Las Vegas-style playground for the people of Milan. He added a minaret, a Chinese-style pagoda, a medieval-style gateway, a grand hotel, shops and dance halls, and crowds came at weekends. But in 1976 a landslide destroyed the only access road, visitors stopped coming, and the resort was gradually abandoned. Today the minaret still rises above crumbling arcades covered in graffiti, and the site has become one of Italy's best-known ghost towns. Local groups organise walks, events and clean-up days, and the hillside is popular with walkers and photographers.

## Getting there

- **Plane:** Milan Bergamo Airport (BGY) is about 35 km away.
- **Train and walk:** trains run from Milan towards Lecco and stop near Olginate; from there it is a steep walk of about an hour up to Consonno.
- **Car:** about 40 minutes from the airport. The last stretch of road is narrow; park below the village and walk up.

## Visiting

The grounds can be walked freely. The buildings are unsafe and should not be entered. Local associations run guided walks and events, especially at weekends. Respect any private property and fenced areas.
