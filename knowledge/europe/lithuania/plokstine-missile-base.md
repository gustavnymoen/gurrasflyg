# Plokštinė missile base

## Overview

The Plokštinė missile base lies in the forests of Žemaitija National Park in western Lithuania. Built in secret by Soviet soldiers in the early 1960s, it was one of the first underground launch sites for nuclear ballistic missiles in the Soviet Union, with four silos for missiles that could reach targets across Western Europe. Local people were told nothing, and the base was hidden under earth mounds and trees. It was taken out of service in 1978 and later abandoned and looted. In 2012 it reopened as the Cold War Museum, where visitors walk through the underground corridors, control rooms and machine halls and can look down into one of the deep silos. Exhibitions tell the story of the arms race and of life in Soviet Lithuania, and the surrounding national park offers lakes and forest trails.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from Palanga International Airport (PLQ), about 50 km, allow 50 minutes. Public transport is limited, with buses to the nearby village of Plateliai.

## Visiting

The museum is open all year with an entry fee, with fewer opening days in winter. Guided tours are available. It is cool underground, so bring a jacket.
