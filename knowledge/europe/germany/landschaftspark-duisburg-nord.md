# Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord

## Overview

Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord is a public park of about 180 hectares built around a former ironworks in the north of Duisburg. The Meiderich ironworks produced pig iron for more than eighty years until it closed in 1985. Rather than clear the site, planners kept the blast furnaces, gas tanks, ore bunkers and railway lines and let nature grow around and through them. Visitors can climb blast furnace 5 to a viewing platform about 70 metres up and look out over the Ruhr area, or walk through the old ore bunkers, which have been turned into gardens. A former gas tank is now a diving centre, and the old concrete walls are used for climbing. At night a coloured light installation turns the ironworks into a work of art. The park is one of the best-known examples of turning an industrial ruin into public space.

## Getting there

- **Train and tram:** from Düsseldorf Airport (DUS), take a train to Duisburg Hauptbahnhof, about 15 minutes, then tram 903 to Landschaftspark Nord.
- **Car:** about 25 minutes from the airport.

## Visiting

The park is open all day and free to enter. Guided tours are available, including evening tours with the light installation. The climb up the blast furnace is on steep metal stairs; wear sturdy shoes.
