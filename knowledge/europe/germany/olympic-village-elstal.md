# Olympic Village 1936

## Overview

The Olympic Village in Elstal, just west of Berlin, housed the male athletes of the 1936 Summer Olympics. Built by the German army, it consisted of more than a hundred low accommodation houses, a large dining hall for the nations, a swimming hall and a gymnasium, set in a landscaped park with a lake and birch woods. The American athlete Jesse Owens, who won four gold medals at the Games, stayed here, and his room has been restored. After the Olympics the village became an army training school, and from 1945 the Soviet army used it as a military camp until its troops left in 1992. Since then many buildings have decayed, while others have been renovated, and new apartments are being built on part of the site. A foundation looks after the historic core of the village and opens it to visitors.

## Getting there

- **Train and bus:** regional trains run to Elstal. From Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) allow about 1–1¼ hours with one change, then take a local bus or walk about 30 minutes.
- **Car:** about 50 minutes from the airport.

## Visiting

The historic part of the village can be visited on guided tours from spring to autumn, mostly at weekends; book ahead. Some buildings are still in poor condition, so stay with the group and on the marked paths.
