# Germany

Destinations:

- [Beelitz-Heilstätten](beelitz-heilstatten.md) — A vast former sanatorium complex near Berlin, partly restored and seen from a treetop walkway. *Access: Open.*
- [Ferropolis](ferropolis.md) — An open-air museum of giant mining excavators on a peninsula in a flooded open-cast mine. *Access: Open.*
- [Landschaftspark Duisburg-Nord](landschaftspark-duisburg-nord.md) — A former ironworks in Duisburg turned into a public park, with a blast furnace you can climb. *Access: Open.*
- [Olympic Village 1936](olympic-village-elstal.md) — The athletes' village of the 1936 Berlin Olympics, later a Soviet barracks, now partly restored. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Prora](prora.md) — A 4.5-kilometre seaside resort on Rügen, built in the 1930s and never used for holidays. *Access: Open.*
- [Spreepark](spreepark.md) — East Berlin's former amusement park, left to decay for two decades and now being restored. *Access: Closed.*
- [Teufelsberg listening station](teufelsberg.md) — A Cold War listening station on a hill of wartime rubble in Berlin, now covered in street art. *Access: Open.*
- [Völklingen Ironworks](volklingen-ironworks.md) — A complete early-20th-century ironworks in Saarland, preserved as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. *Access: Open.*
- [Wünsdorf (the 'Forbidden City')](wunsdorf.md) — The former headquarters of the Soviet forces in East Germany, once a closed military town. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Zollverein Coal Mine](zollverein.md) — A vast former coal mine and coking plant in Essen, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site. *Access: Open.*
