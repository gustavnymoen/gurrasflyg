# Bunk'Art

## Overview

Bunk'Art is a museum and art space inside a vast nuclear bunker on the outskirts of Tirana, the capital of Albania. It was built in the 1970s for the dictator Enver Hoxha and the leadership of the communist regime, which isolated Albania from the rest of the world and filled the country with hundreds of thousands of small concrete bunkers out of fear of invasion. The five-storey underground complex contains more than a hundred rooms, including offices, living quarters and an assembly hall, designed to keep the government running after a nuclear attack. It was never used for that purpose, and for years after the fall of communism it was forgotten. In 2014 it opened to the public as Bunk'Art, with exhibitions about the history of communist Albania and contemporary art. A second site, Bunk'Art 2, in a smaller bunker in central Tirana, focuses on the secret police.

## Getting there

- **Taxi:** from Tirana International Airport (TIA), about 20 km, allow 30 minutes to Bunk'Art 1.
- **Bus:** local buses from central Tirana towards the Dajti mountain pass near the entrance.

## Visiting

Bunk'Art is open daily with an entry fee; allow about two hours. It is cool underground. The exhibitions describe repression and persecution, and some of the content is distressing.
