# France

Destinations:

- [Oradour-sur-Glane](oradour-sur-glane.md) — A French village kept in ruins as a memorial to the massacre of its inhabitants in June 1944. *Access: Open.*
- [Ouvrage Hackenberg (Maginot Line)](ouvrage-hackenberg.md) — The largest fort of the Maginot Line, a 1930s underground fortress with its own electric railway. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Paris Catacombs](paris-catacombs.md) — An underground ossuary in old quarries beneath Paris, holding the remains of millions of Parisians. *Access: Open.*
- [Petite Ceinture](petite-ceinture.md) — An abandoned railway ring around Paris, parts of which have been opened as green walkways. *Access: Open.*
