# United Kingdom

Destinations:

- [Imber](imber.md) — A Wiltshire village evacuated in 1943 for military training and opened to the public only on a few days a year. *Access: Open on set days.*
- [Maunsell Sea Forts](maunsell-sea-forts.md) — Second World War anti-aircraft forts on stilts in the Thames Estuary, seen on boat trips. *Access: Exterior only.*
- [Orford Ness](orford-ness.md) — A remote shingle spit in Suffolk with the ruins of secret military test sites, run by the National Trust. *Access: Open on set days.*
- [St Kilda](st-kilda.md) — A remote Scottish archipelago whose last islanders left in 1930, reached by boat in summer. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Tyneham](tyneham.md) — A Dorset village taken over by the army in 1943 and left empty ever since, open on most weekends. *Access: Open on set days.*
