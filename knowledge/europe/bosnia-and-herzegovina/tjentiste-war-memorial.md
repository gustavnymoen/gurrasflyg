# Tjentište War Memorial

## Overview

The Tjentište War Memorial stands in the valley of the Sutjeska river in Sutjeska National Park, in south-eastern Bosnia and Herzegovina. It commemorates the Battle of the Sutjeska in 1943, when Yugoslav Partisans led by Josip Broz Tito broke out of an encirclement by German and Italian forces at the cost of thousands of lives. Completed in 1971 and designed by the sculptor Miodrag Živković, the memorial consists of two huge, fractured concrete forms facing each other, said to represent the breakthrough. It is one of the best known of the Yugoslav memorials called spomeniks, abstract monuments built across the country in the decades after the war. A memorial hall with large frescoes and a cemetery for the fallen lie nearby. Set against forested mountains, the site is both a place of remembrance and a striking piece of modernist sculpture.

## Getting there

- **Car:** from Sarajevo International Airport (SJJ), about 110 km, allow two hours via Foča.
- **Bus:** some buses between Sarajevo and the south of the country pass Tjentište; check timetables before you travel.

## Visiting

The memorial is freely accessible all year; the memorial hall's opening hours vary. It is a war memorial and should be visited respectfully. A visit combines well with hiking in Sutjeska National Park.
