# Sarajevo bobsleigh track

## Overview

The bobsleigh and luge track on Mount Trebević was built for the 1984 Winter Olympics in Sarajevo, then part of Yugoslavia, the first Winter Games held in a socialist country. The concrete track, about 1.3 km long with a series of banked curves, winds down through the pine forest above the city. During the siege of Sarajevo in the 1990s the mountain lay on the front line: Bosnian Serb forces used the track as an artillery position, and the surrounding forest was mined. After the war the track was left to decay. Today it is covered with layers of colourful graffiti and street art, and walking along its curves among the trees is one of the most popular outings from Sarajevo. Since the Trebević cable car was rebuilt in 2018, the mountain is once again easy to reach from the old town.

## Getting there

- **Taxi:** from Sarajevo International Airport (SJJ), about 15 km away, take a taxi into the city, about 20 minutes, or directly up the mountain, about 30 minutes.
- **Cable car and walk:** the cable car from the old town reaches the top of Trebević in about 10 minutes; from there, walk about 20 minutes to the track.

## Visiting

The track is freely accessible. Landmines have been cleared from the area, but stay on the track and on well-used paths. The concrete can be slippery after rain, and there is snow on the mountain in winter.
