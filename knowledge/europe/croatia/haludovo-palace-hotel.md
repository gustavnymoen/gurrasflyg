# Haludovo Palace Hotel

## Overview

The Haludovo Palace Hotel stands on the shore of the Adriatic at Malinska on the Croatian island of Krk. It opened in the early 1970s as one of the most luxurious resorts in Yugoslavia, with a striking modernist main building, a "fishing village" of guest bungalows and a casino financed by Bob Guccione, the American founder of Penthouse magazine. The casino soon failed, but the hotel continued to receive guests, many of them from abroad, until the wars of the 1990s, when tourism collapsed and the buildings housed refugees. After a troubled privatisation the complex was left empty, and its glass, concrete and swimming pools slowly decayed. Today the hotel's ruins are a well-known destination for urban explorers and photographers. Investors have been planning a renovation for years, but the future of the site is still undecided.

## Getting there

- **Car or taxi:** Rijeka Airport (RJK) is on the island of Krk itself, about 10 km away; allow about 15 minutes.

## Visiting

The buildings are closed, unstable and private property, so the hotel can only be seen from outside, from the coastal path and the beach. The bay nearby is good for swimming in summer.
