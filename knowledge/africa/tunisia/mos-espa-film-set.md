# Mos Espa film set

## Overview

The Mos Espa film set stands among sand dunes in the desert of southern Tunisia, near the rock formation of Ong Jemel, about 30 km from the oasis town of Tozeur. It was built in 1997 for the film Star Wars: Episode I, The Phantom Menace, as the town on the desert planet Tatooine where the young Anakin Skywalker lives, and it was used again for a later film in the series. After filming, the crew left the set behind. Its domed houses, arches, towers and fake machinery, made from plaster and wood, still stand in the sand, weathered by the sun and wind. For years, moving dunes threatened to bury the set, and parts of it have been repaired over the years so that it can stay open to visitors. Today local traders sell souvenirs among the buildings, and the area attracts both film fans and tourists exploring the Sahara. Ong Jemel itself appeared in the film The English Patient.

## Getting there

- **4x4 tour:** from Tozeur-Nefta International Airport (TOE), about 35 km, organised 4x4 trips take about an hour over desert tracks.
- **Plane:** Tozeur has few flights; most international visitors fly to Tunis-Carthage (TUN) and continue by domestic flight or a long drive.

## Visiting

The set is open to walk around, and local guides and traders are usually on site. It is very hot in summer and there is no shade, so go early in the morning or near sunset, and bring water. Do not drive into the dunes without a guide and a suitable vehicle.
