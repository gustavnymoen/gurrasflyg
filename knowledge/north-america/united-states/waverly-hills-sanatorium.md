# Waverly Hills Sanatorium

## Overview

Waverly Hills Sanatorium stands on a wooded hill on the south-western edge of Louisville, Kentucky. It opened as a small tuberculosis hospital in 1910, when the disease was spreading fast in the area, and in 1926 a large new five-storey building replaced it, able to treat around 400 patients. Treatment relied on rest, fresh air and sunlight, with patients wheeled out onto long open porches in all weathers. Before antibiotics, many did not recover, and the hospital used a tunnel down the hill, later nicknamed the "body chute", to take away the bodies of those who died out of sight of the other patients. After new drugs made the sanatorium unnecessary, it closed in 1961 and later served as a care home until the early 1980s. Abandoned and vandalised for years, it gained a reputation as one of the most haunted places in the United States. Today its owners run history and paranormal tours.

## Getting there

- **Taxi or car:** from Louisville Muhammad Ali International Airport (SDF), about 15 km, allow 20 minutes.

## Visiting

Visits are on guided tours only, from history tours to overnight paranormal investigations; book ahead. The tours involve many stairs. Many patients died here, so please visit respectfully.
