# United States

Destinations:

- [Alcatraz](alcatraz.md) — The former federal prison on an island in San Francisco Bay, reached by ferry. *Access: Open.*
- [Bannack](bannack.md) — Montana's first territorial capital, now a ghost town preserved as a state park. *Access: Open.*
- [Bannerman Castle](bannerman-castle.md) — The ruins of a mock-Scottish castle built as an arsenal on a tiny island in the Hudson River. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Bodie](bodie.md) — A Gold Rush ghost town in the hills of eastern California, preserved in a state of 'arrested decay'. *Access: Open.*
- [City Hall Station](city-hall-station.md) — New York's original showpiece subway station from 1904, closed in 1945 and preserved under City Hall. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Eastern State Penitentiary](eastern-state-penitentiary.md) — A vast 19th-century prison in Philadelphia, preserved as a ruin and open as a museum. *Access: Open.*
- [Ellis Island Hospital](ellis-island-hospital.md) — The abandoned immigrant hospital on Ellis Island in New York Harbor, visited on hard-hat tours. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Garnet](garnet.md) — A remote gold-mining ghost town in the mountains of western Montana. *Access: Open.*
- [Kennecott](kennecott.md) — A red copper-mill town at the end of a remote road in Alaska's Wrangell-St. Elias National Park. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Ohio State Reformatory](ohio-state-reformatory.md) — A castle-like prison in Mansfield, Ohio, famous as the main filming location of The Shawshank Redemption. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Old Cahawba](old-cahawba.md) — The site of Alabama's first state capital, now a ghost town of ruins, columns and old cemeteries. *Access: Open.*
- [Pennhurst State School and Hospital](pennhurst.md) — A former state institution in Pennsylvania whose history of abuse led to landmark disability-rights rulings. *Access: Open on set days.*
- [Rhyolite](rhyolite.md) — A ruined Nevada gold-rush town near Death Valley, with a bottle house and an open-air sculpture museum. *Access: Open.*
- [Salton Sea and Bombay Beach](salton-sea-bombay-beach.md) — A shrinking desert lake in California lined with abandoned resorts, and an art scene in Bombay Beach. *Access: Open.*
- [Sloss Furnaces](sloss-furnaces.md) — A preserved iron-making plant in Birmingham, Alabama, now a museum and metal-arts centre. *Access: Open.*
- [St. Elmo](st-elmo.md) — One of Colorado's best-preserved mining ghost towns, high in the Rocky Mountains. *Access: Open.*
- [Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum](trans-allegheny-lunatic-asylum.md) — A huge 19th-century sandstone asylum in West Virginia, open for history and ghost tours. *Access: Guided tour.*
- [Waverly Hills Sanatorium](waverly-hills-sanatorium.md) — A former tuberculosis hospital in Louisville, Kentucky, known for its 'body chute' and ghost tours. *Access: Guided tour.*
