# TRIP-005 Routes, departures and aircraft assignment

> **Superseded in part by TRIP-010 (2026-10-23):** every pair of airports now has a route with at least 5 departures a day (TRIP-010-R10), and routes have no aircraft, so "Tilldela plan" is gone (TRIP-010-R12). The requirements below are kept as history.

## Goal

The airline has a realistic route network: a couple of hundred routes in both directions, with flight times that match the distance and a few departures a day. The administrator can give every route an aircraft with one click.

## Background

Built before specification-driven development was introduced and specified afterwards. The routes are generated sample data for the airline's own flight booking. They are separate from the course's timetables (`data/flights.json`), which belong to charter.

Routes and departures describe when flights *can* run. Turning them into bookable flights on dates is a later feature.

## Users

Administrator. Travellers meet the routes later, in search results.

## Scope

**In scope**

- Airports, routes and daily departure times in the database (migrations `001_routes.sql` and `002_routes_data.sql`, generated by `scripts/generate-routes.mjs`).
- The button "Tilldela plan" on `/admin`.

**Out of scope**

- Flights on dates, prices and seats (later feature).
- Editing routes by hand in admin.

## Requirements

### TRIP-005-R1 Airports

The database shall have 51 airports: Swedish and Nordic airports, large European airports and a few long-haul destinations (for example New York JFK and Bangkok), each with IATA code, name, country and time zone.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** a new database, **then** it has 51 airports, including ARN, GOT, LHR, JFK and BKK.

### TRIP-005-R2 Routes in both directions

The database shall have 200 routes: 100 airport pairs, each flown in both directions with the same flight time. No route is shorter than 150 km. Routes over 4,000 km always start or end at a large hub airport. Every airport has at least one route, and the obvious routes for a Swedish airline exist (for example ARN–GOT, ARN–LHR and ARN–JFK).

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** a new database, **then** there are 200 routes, every route has a return route with the same flight time, and every airport has a route.
- **Then** ARN–GOT, ARN–LHR and ARN–JFK exist in both directions.

### TRIP-005-R3 Realistic flight times

A route's flight time shall be the distance at 800 km/h, rounded to 5 minutes, plus 30 minutes for taxiing, take-off and landing.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Then** Stockholm Arlanda–New York JFK takes 500 minutes (8 h 20 min), Stockholm Arlanda–London Heathrow 140 minutes and Stockholm Arlanda–Göteborg 60 minutes.

### TRIP-005-R4 Departures

Every route shall have 2 or 3 departures a day, between 06:00 and 21:30 local time at the departure airport, at least 3 hours apart, on whole 5 minutes.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Then** every route has 2–3 departure times, all between 06:00 and 21:30, at least 180 minutes apart.

### TRIP-005-R5 Reproducible generation

The routes shall be generated by `scripts/generate-routes.mjs` with a fixed random seed, so the same run always gives the same result. Its output is the migration `db/migrations/002_routes_data.sql`, which isn't edited by hand.

Acceptance criteria:

- **When** `node scripts/generate-routes.mjs` is run, **then** its output is identical to `db/migrations/002_routes_data.sql`.

### TRIP-005-R6 Assignment status

The admin page shall show the button "Tilldela plan" in the top right corner, and under it "<n> av <total> rutter har plan".

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** a signed-in administrator, **then** the button and the status line are shown.

### TRIP-005-R7 Assign aircraft

"Tilldela plan" shall give every route a randomly chosen aircraft, replacing earlier assignments. Afterwards the status line shows that all routes have an aircraft, and the column Rutter in the aircraft table shows how many routes each aircraft flies.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** one aircraft, **when** "Tilldela plan" is pressed, **then** "200 av 200 rutter har plan" is shown, and the aircraft has 200 routes.

### TRIP-005-R8 No aircraft to assign

Without aircraft, "Tilldela plan" shall show "Det finns inga plan att tilldela. Lägg till ett plan först." and change nothing.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** no aircraft, **when** "Tilldela plan" is pressed, **then** the message is shown.

### TRIP-005-R9 Deleted aircraft leave their routes

When an aircraft is deleted, the routes it flew shall be left without an aircraft.

Acceptance criteria:

- **Given** all routes assigned to one aircraft, **when** it is deleted, **then** "0 av 200 rutter har plan" is shown.

## Data

- `airports` (from `db/seed.sql` and the migration), `routes` (from, to, flight time in minutes, aircraft, unique per direction) and `route_departures` (route, time `HH:MM`).
- Deleting an aircraft sets `routes.aircraft_id` to null.

## Interface

- `app/admin/AssignAircraftButton.tsx`, the action `assignAircraftAction`, and `getRouteStats` and `assignAircraftToRoutes` in `lib/admin.ts`.

## Non-functional requirements

- Assigning 200 routes is done in one batch in one transaction, so it is fast against Turso and all-or-nothing.

## Notes on testing

R1–R5, R7, R8 and R9 are tested only against the local test database (`npm run test:local`): R1–R5 read the database directly, and R7–R9 would replace the real assignments on the live site.
